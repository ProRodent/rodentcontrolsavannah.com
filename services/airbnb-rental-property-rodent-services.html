Unlike standard residential work, rental programs are structured around guest-arrival deadlines, online-review risk, and the need for invisible service that doesn’t disrupt occupied units. Why this matters in Savannah

Savannah’s short-term rental market is among the densest in the Southeast. Tybee Island’s beach cottages, the Historic District’s carriage houses and converted townhomes, downtown short-stay studios, and the SCAD-area student rentals during semester transitions. Each operates on a weekly or monthly turnover cycle, and each has a unique rodent risk profile.

The defining issue for rental properties is the gap between guest stays. A vacation rental sits empty for 12-48 hours between guests during peak season. That’s the window for rodent treatment, cleanup, and any exclusion work. Outside that window, a guest is sleeping in the property and any service that happens is disruption. Programs built for owner-occupied homes don’t accommodate that constraint; rental programs are built around it. Tybee Island in particular has an additional layer: elevated beach construction creates large vented under-house spaces that house Norway rats and mice if not sealed, and the salt-air corrosion environment limits which materials hold up long-term.

The other rental-specific factor is online reviews. A single guest review mentioning rodents stays on Airbnb, VRBO, and Google indefinitely and reduces bookings for the life of the listing. The cost of preventive monthly service is trivial against the cost of a single negative review. Rental owners who understand this run preventive programs; those who learn it the hard way usually call us after the first bad review.

Frequently asked questions

Can you work around a guest who’s currently occupying the rental?

For preventive service, yes: most of our work is exterior and quarterly visits happen during scheduled turnover gaps anyway. For active guest events, we can sometimes work with the guest present (typically by coordinating with the property manager) but rarely; most active-event work waits for the property to be vacant, which usually means a same-day window after checkout.

How fast can you respond to an active guest event?

Same-day during operating hours (9AM-9PM). Rental accounts route to priority dispatch ahead of general residential. Most events get a treatment slot the same afternoon or evening. Important because turnover windows are tight and guest-arrival schedules don’t move.

Will the bait stations be visible to my guests?

Exterior stations are placed in non-guest-visible locations: along foundation lines hidden by landscaping, behind HVAC units, in service areas. Interior monitoring stations are placed in utility closets, behind appliances, or in attic/crawl access only. Guests typically never see them.

Do you handle Tybee Island vacation cottages specifically?

Yes. Tybee Island is one of our highest-volume rental service areas. Tybee cottages have specific considerations: elevated construction (vented under-house space), salt-air corrosion (hardware material selection), tropical-storm season (October-November scheduling around weather), and high summer turnover. The island follows a consistent seasonal pattern.

What about Historic District short-term rentals?

Yes. Historic District carriage houses, converted townhomes, and townhouse short-stay units are regular service areas. These typically need restoration-friendly exclusion (see our historic home rodent control service) combined with rental-cycle scheduling. We can do both as one program.

How does this work with a property manager handling multiple rentals?

Multi-property accounts get consolidated reporting, single point of contact, and group pricing if applicable. Property managers handling 5+ rentals across Savannah often use us for the whole portfolio with monthly group invoicing. Direct dispatch line for the manager.

Will you provide documentation for an Airbnb dispute about rodents?

Yes. Written reports for guest events include date, location, findings, treatment performed, and verification of clearance. Can support Airbnb’s host-protected dispute documentation, VRBO claims, and platform refund-prevention contexts.

What about pre-purchase rodent inspection for a property I’m considering buying as a rental?

We do inspection-only service. See our rodent inspection services for that scope. Pre-purchase rodent inspection on a Savannah rental candidate is highly recommended given the housing stock and the rental-review risk. A 1-hour inspection saves significant downstream cost on the wrong property.

How are rental programs different from standard residential rodent control?

Three things: scheduling (turnover windows instead of homeowner availability), documentation (every visit produces records for owner/platform use), and response priority (rental events go to the front of the dispatch queue because of guest-arrival deadlines). The treatment work itself is similar to residential; the program structure is different.
