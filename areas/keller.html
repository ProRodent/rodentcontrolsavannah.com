What species am I most likely to see in Keller?

House mice are common across rural properties. Norway rats appear at agricultural properties. Roof rats appear in established residential. Is the work the same quality as your Savannah city work?

Yes. Inspection, materials and follow-up are identical to what a Savannah address gets. The same 90-day warranty on exclusion work applies, and the longer drive is the only difference.

Do you handle commercial properties in Keller?

Yes. Keller businesses are covered by our commercial programs, as is the rest of Bryan County. We work with restaurants, retail shops, warehouses, healthcare facilities and offices. We document each visit so the paperwork is there if an inspector asks. The exact requirements depend on the facility.

Are your treatments safe with pets and children?

Yes, and the method is chosen with them in mind. Traps and covered stations are the default indoors, particularly in households with animals or kids. Outdoor tamper-resistant stations are the only place bait is used, and only when needed. Tell us if you would rather skip it. Nothing goes on the property without an explanation first.
